01Information We Collect and Process

Under Hatch can be used without creating a separate account. However, the following information may be processed to provide Google Play Games sign-in (Android), leaderboard and battle server records, advertising, in-app purchase, and app update features.

1) Google Play Games Services (Android only)

You may optionally sign in with your Google Play account. If you sign in or use Play Games features, the following information may be processed:

  • Google Play Games player ID
  • Display name of your Google account or in-game nickname
  • Player profile and profile image
  • Captain name, in-game currency, stage clear records, settings, and other game progress data

Game progress data may be stored both in local storage on your device and in Google Play Games cloud storage, and is used to back up, sync, and restore your progress when you change devices or reinstall the app.

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. Without signing in, you can still use the basic features of the game, except for cloud saves and cross-device sync.

The iOS version does not offer Google Play Games sign-in or cloud saves. On iOS, game progress data is stored only on your device, and there is no account sign-in.

Personal information processed by Google Play Games Services is separately subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • How to delete Google Play Games data and profile

2) Google AdMob Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads only. Rewarded ads play only when you tap a button yourself, such as Emergency Repair or Double Reward; the app does not show banner or interstitial ads. In the course of serving ads, Google and its partner ad networks may automatically collect or process the following information:

  • Advertising identifier — the Android Advertising ID or the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). The IDFA is used only if you allow tracking in response to the request described below.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device type, model, operating system, and language settings
  • App version and ad SDK information
  • Ad impressions, clicks, view completion, and reward status
  • App launch and ad interaction information
  • Technical information needed for ad fraud prevention and security
  • Error, diagnostic, and performance information

This information may be processed to serve ads, limit ad frequency, provide personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, prevent ad fraud, and improve the service.

Ad content rating — For all users, the app limits ad content to a maximum rating of PG (parental guidance). Mature or suggestive ads (T or MA ratings) are not requested.

iOS tracking request (App Tracking Transparency) — On iOS, Apple's tracking permission prompt is shown the first time you launch the app. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not provided; ads will still be shown, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — Users in these regions are asked whether they consent to personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) the first time they launch the app. You can still play the game if you decline; in that case, only limited, non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

Depending on your age, region, consent status, and device settings, personalized ads may be shown. You can opt out of personalized ads or reset/delete your advertising identifier through Google My Ad Center, the ad settings on your Android device, or the tracking settings on your iPhone.

Menu names may vary by device and Android version, but these settings are generally found at:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Ads privacy)
  • Turn off ads personalization
  • Reset or delete advertising ID
  • iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off tracking for this app
  • Google My Ad Center
  • Google Privacy Policy

Even if you turn off ads personalization, ads will still be shown, but they may be non-personalized ads that are not based on your interests.

3) In-App Purchases (Google Play · Apple App Store)

You can purchase digital goods such as diamonds, passes, and bundles through the Google Play billing system on Android or Apple App Store in-app purchases on iOS.

Payments and payment methods are handled directly by Google Play or Apple. The developer does not access or store your payment method information, such as credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or payment passwords.

The following minimal purchase information may be processed to verify purchases, deliver items, restore purchases, prevent duplicate delivery, handle refunds, and prevent fraudulent transactions:

  • Product ID
  • Order number or transaction ID
  • Purchase token
  • Date and time of purchase
  • Payment and purchase status
  • Cancellation, refund, or revocation status
  • Whether the item has been delivered and consumed

Purchase verification server — To confirm that a purchase is valid, right after the purchase the app sends the following information to a verification server operated by the developer (Google Cloud Functions, Seoul region):

  • Purchase token (Android) or the Apple-signed transaction information (iOS)
  • Product ID, product type, app package name / bundle ID, and platform (Android/iOS)

The verification server checks this information with Google Play or by verifying Apple's signature and returns only the result (whether the item can be delivered) to the app. It does not store the information it receives. Only when an error occurs may the product ID and the error type be recorded in the server logs to help diagnose the cause; purchase tokens and transaction information are never logged. The delivery record (the purchase ledger used to prevent duplicate delivery) is stored only on your device.

4) Game Progress Data

The following data may be generated as you play:

  • Captain name (an in-game nickname you enter yourself)
  • In-game currency such as coins, diamonds, steel plates, and parts, and owned items
  • Turret and ship upgrade information
  • Stage clear records and Hell difficulty records
  • Pass progress and reward claim records
  • Patrol reward and daily gift claim records
  • Game settings

Game progress data is stored in local storage on your device. On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, it may also be stored in Google Play Games storage for cloud backup and cross-device sync.

The captain name is a nickname used only within the game and is stored on your device. However, if you use the leaderboard or battle features, it is also stored on the server described in 5) below and shown to other users. On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, it may also be backed up to the cloud together with your other game progress data. We recommend that you do not enter personal information, such as your real name or contact details, as your captain name.

5) Leaderboard and Battle Server (Google Firebase)

To manage records and rankings for Ranked, Endless, and Battle modes, the app uses a server operated by the developer (Google Firebase: Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions). You do not need to sign in or register separately to use these features; the app automatically creates an anonymous account without any name or contact details. In this process, the following information is stored on the server:

  • Anonymous account number (the identifier assigned by Firebase Anonymous Authentication) and a game ID generated by the app on your device
  • Captain name (a nickname you enter yourself, up to 12 characters)
  • Ranked and Endless records, such as stage clear records, damage taken, clear time, and number of attempts
  • Battle trophies, combat power, and the fleet setup used in battles (weapons, upgrade levels) and skin information
  • Feature usage records managed by the server, such as the number of daily battles, arena points, and whether the weekly reward has been granted
  • Communication records sent with server requests, such as your IP address (processed by Google; the developer does not use them to identify users)

Who can see it — Your captain name and your stage, trophy, combat power, and ranking information are shown to other users on the leaderboard. In battles, your captain name and fleet setup may be used as an opponent for other users and shown to them. The anonymous account number and game ID are not shown on the app screen, but they are included in the server documents because of how the leaderboard data is structured. Your real name, contact details, payment information, and advertising identifier are not stored on this server.

Ranking and battle records are managed by the server, and the app is restricted from writing to the server directly. Past records of the weekly leaderboard are preserved so that they can be viewed as this week's and last week's rankings. If you delete and reinstall the app, a new anonymous account is created and new records appear on the leaderboard; the records of the previous account may remain until you request their deletion.

6) App Update Check (Expo)

When launched, the app checks whether a new update to the game content (code and images) is available and, if so, downloads and applies it. This feature uses the update servers of Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), and the following information is sent in the process:

  • IP address
  • Platform (Android/iOS), app version, and the identifier of the currently applied update

This information is used only to deliver the appropriate update, and the developer does not use it to identify users.

04Children's Privacy

Under Hatch has no account registration and does not ask for information that directly identifies users, such as name, contact details, or date of birth. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14. The app does not have a separate age verification process.

For all users, ad content is limited to a maximum rating of PG (parental guidance) so that mature or suggestive ads are not shown. The only ads in the app are rewarded ads, which play only when the user taps a button.

Parents and guardians can turn off personalized ads on a child's device through the device's ad settings (deleting the Android Advertising ID, or turning off tracking permission on iPhone).

If we learn that personal information of a child under 14 has been processed without the consent of a legal guardian, we will take necessary measures, such as deleting that information without delay.

A child's legal guardian may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of the child's personal information by contacting modoolabs1730@gmail.com.
